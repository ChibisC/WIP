# Review rubric: hell ants and nasute termites (realism rework, 2026-11-02)

This rubric is fixed for every round (spec §2, §6). Each round uses a fresh reviewer who hasn't seen the earlier rounds. The reviewer gets:
- the images under neutral names (`img-01` …), with real photos mixed in among the renders;
- the three calibration anchors below, already scored.

The reviewer is told only that some images are photographs and some are renders. They score each image without knowing which is which.

## Scale (0–10, per image)

| Score | Means |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | Not recognisable as the animal |
| 2–3 | Clean CG: smooth plastic surfaces, uniform tubes, toy colours, clearly a render at a glance (**anchor A = 3**) |
| 4 | A good game asset: correct anatomy, some surface detail, but waxy or plastic and lit like a model (**anchor B = 4**) |
| 5–6 | Convincing at a glance; up close the materials, hairs or lighting give it away |
| 7 | Could pass as a macro photo at sheet scale; one or two tells on inspection (**the target**) |
| 8–9 | Indistinguishable from a photo except for small details (**anchor C = 9**: a real macro photograph) |
| 10 | A photograph |

## Five aspects (each 0–10; the image's score is their mean, rounded to 0.5)

1. **Shape**: the silhouette and proportions against the species (head, horn and jaws or nasus, waist or none, legs, antennae).
2. **Surface**: gloss where it belongs and dull where not; microsculpture, sutures and segment margins; hairs; soft parts translucent.
3. **Colour**: believable pigment and value range; the jaws or nasus read against the body; nothing toy-saturated.
4. **Detail at sheet scale**: joints, segments, setae, eyes and claws readable without looking like noise.
5. **Light and ground**: the subject sits on the ground with contact shadows, takes the scene's light, and gives no studio or plastic tells.

For each image the reviewer also names **the single biggest tell**: the one thing that most gives it away as a render.

## Anchors (the same three images every round)

- **A (3):** `before/hell_worker_side_overcast.png`, the hell ant worker before this rework.
- **B (4):** `anchors/fire_minor_side_overcast.png`, the game's previous best realism ant, a fire ant worker. Earlier blind reviews scored it 3–4.
- **C (9):** a real macro photograph of a live nasute soldier from the user's study photos. It is study-only and is never added to the repo.

## Units and views per round

- **Units:** hell worker, soldier and queen; nasute worker, soldier, major, king and queen.
- **Views:** side and three-quarter under overcast light, plus the macro close-up under forest shade.
- **Photos mixed in:** 3–4 of the user's photos, which are study-only and never added to the repo.

**Target:** a mean of 7 per unit. Scores and fixes for every round are recorded in PROGRESS.md.
