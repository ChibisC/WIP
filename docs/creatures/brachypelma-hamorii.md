# Mexican red-knee tarantula — *Brachypelma hamorii* (Theraphosidae) — BOSS

## Size
- Body 55–70 mm, leg span up to 150 mm (game boss: body 90 mm, ~1.4×).

## Anatomy
- Heavy **cephalothorax** (≈ .45 L) with a small eye tubercle bearing 8 tiny
  eyes clustered together (poor vision); radiating grooves.
- Large rounded **abdomen** (≈ .55 L), very hairy, with a patch of
  **urticating hairs** on the back; two pairs of spinnerets (posterior long,
  finger-like).
- Large **chelicerae** in front with long curved black fangs pointing
  down/back (mygalomorph: fangs strike downward, parallel).
- Thick, very hairy legs and pedipalps; scopulae pads on tarsi.

## Colour (diagnostic)
- Body and legs **velvety black/dark brown**; **patellae ("knees") bright
  orange-red**, with orange rings on the distal tibia and metatarsus;
  carapace with a black centre and a beige-orange edge; abdomen black with
  longer reddish-brown hairs. Dense hair → strong fuzz/sheen.

## Movement
- Slow deliberate walk (alternating tetrapod), long pauses, then sudden
  forward lunges.

## Behaviour (boss phases)
1. **Threat posture** (telegraph): raises the front legs and pedipalps and
   lifts the cephalothorax, showing fangs → then **lunges** with a downward
   fang strike (venom).
2. **Hair flick**: turns and rubs the abdomen with a hind leg, flicking
   **urticating hairs** in a cloud → area debuff (slowed, reduced damage).
3. **Burrow ambush / frenzy**: low hp → rapid lunges and body slams.

## Facts for info cards
- New World tarantulas defend themselves by flicking barbed urticating hairs from their abdomen.
- Female red-knee tarantulas can live 25–30 years.
- Tarantulas' fangs strike downward, not pinching sideways like most spiders.
