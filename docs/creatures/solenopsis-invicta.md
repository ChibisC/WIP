# Red imported fire ant — *Solenopsis invicta* (Myrmicinae)

## Size
- Workers polymorphic, 2.4–6 mm (minors ~2.5 mm, majors ~5–6 mm).
  Queen ~7–8 mm with a massive gaster when established.

## Anatomy and proportions
- Minor (L = 2.8 mm): head .25 L, slightly longer than wide; major
  (L = 5.5 mm): head .32 L, **disproportionately large**, broader behind.
- Eyes moderate with many facets. Clypeus with a median tooth (ID feature).
- Antennae **10 segments with a 2-segmented club** (last two segments
  enlarged, clearly visible).
- Mandibles with 4 teeth.
- Mesosoma .30 L, smooth, **no propodeal spines**.
- Waist: petiole + postpetiole (two rounded nodes), .10 L.
- Gaster .35 L, oval, **sting** visible at the tip.
- Legs moderate: hind leg ≈ .85 L.

## Colour and surface
- Head and mesosoma **reddish-brown / coppery**; gaster **dark brown to
  black**, sometimes with a faint basal yellowish spot; legs reddish-brown.
- Shiny, unsculptured, with abundant thin erect hairs.

## Behaviour
- Extremely numerous and aggressive; boils out of the mound when disturbed.
- **Stinging**: grips the skin/prey with the mandibles, then arches the gaster
  down and stings, **pivoting around the bite** to sting repeatedly in a
  circle. Venom is mostly piperidine alkaloids (solenopsins) → necrotic,
  burning pain. → Game: venom stacks (DoT) and slow-acting but deadly swarms.
- **Mounds** of worked soil with **no central entrance** — workers use
  subterranean foraging tunnels that open away from the mound.
- Survives floods by linking bodies into floating **rafts** with the queen
  and brood inside.
- Polygyne forms have many queens per mound.

## Facts for info cards
- Fire ants survive floods by forming waterproof living rafts that can float for weeks.
- Their venom is mostly alkaloids called solenopsins, unusual among ants.
- S. invicta arrived in Alabama in the 1930s and is now on the list of the world's worst invasive species.
