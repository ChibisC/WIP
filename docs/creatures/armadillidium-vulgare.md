# Common pill woodlouse — *Armadillidium vulgare* (Isopoda, Crustacea)

## Size
- Up to 18 mm (game: 12 mm).

## Anatomy
- **Domed, oval** body (strongly convex), head with short antennae (the
  second pair visible, 7 segments in two parts) and compound eyes on the sides.
- **Seven overlapping pereon plates** (thoracic tergites), then a short
  tapering pleon and a **triangular telson** that fits flush when rolled.
- **Seven pairs of walking legs** (pereopods), short, all similar.
- Able to roll into a perfect **ball (conglobation)**.

## Colour
- Slate grey to blue-black, slightly glossy, often with faint pale flecks
  along plate edges (females mottled with yellow spots). Satiny clearcoat.

## Movement
- Steady **metachronal leg waves** (back to front) — smooth gliding walk,
  antennae tapping ahead.

## Behaviour
- Detritivore, harmless. When touched or attacked **rolls into a ball**,
  protecting legs and underside (huge armour while rolled), unrolls after a
  pause and walks away.
- Game: passive creature that becomes food; rolled state = high armour.

## Facts for info cards
- Woodlice are crustaceans — closer relatives of crabs and shrimp than of insects.
- They breathe with gill-like structures and need damp places to survive.
