# Large yellow underwing caterpillar (cutworm) — *Noctua pronuba* larva (Noctuidae)

## Size
- Up to 50 mm (game: 40 mm).

## Anatomy
- Cylindrical soft body: head capsule (≈ .07 L, rounded, with
  stemmata and short antennae, mandibles) + 3 thoracic segments with
  **3 pairs of true jointed legs** + 10 abdominal segments with
  **prolegs on A3–A6 (4 pairs) and anal prolegs on A10** (crochets).
- Spiracles visible as dots along each side.

## Colour
- Green-brown to grey-brown; a row of **black dashes** along each side of the
  back (dorsolateral) on the rear segments, pale lateral line, darker
  speckling; head tan with two dark stripes. Matte, slightly glossy skin.

## Movement
- **Peristaltic wave** moving from the tail to the head: prolegs lift and step
  forward in sequence while the body segment contracts; true legs step at the
  front. Slow and steady.

## Behaviour
- Nocturnal; hides in soil by day; feeds on low plants.
- Defence: **curls into a tight C-shape** when disturbed, thrashes the head.
- Game: slow passive creature, big food reward; curls when attacked.

## Facts for info cards
- Cutworms are named for chewing through plant stems at ground level at night.
- Caterpillars have only six true legs; the rest are fleshy prolegs with tiny hooks.
