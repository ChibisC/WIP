# Giant Asian mantis — *Hierodula membranacea* (Mantidae) — BOSS

## Size
- Females 80–100 mm (game boss: 130 mm, ~1.4×).

## Anatomy (differences from Mantis religiosa)
- Heavier, broader build; prothorax shorter and stouter relative to the body;
  wider wings/tegmina covering the abdomen; **forelegs robust with yellowish
  tubercles at the base of the coxae** instead of the black eyespot; the inner
  forecoxa often has pale bumps.
- Each tegmen has a small **white stigma spot** near the middle of the front edge.
- Large rounded eyes, triangular head.

## Colour
- Bright leaf green to brown; tegmina slightly translucent with veins; white
  stigma; satin.

## Behaviour (boss phases)
1. **Stillness and sway** — becomes nearly invisible (camouflage), sways;
   telegraph: head locks on target, body cocks back, forelegs open (0.6 s).
   Strike sweeps a long cone and snatches the strongest ant.
2. **Deimatic display** — rears up, spreads wings: all ants in front are
   stunned (fear), then a double strike.
3. **Hunting walk** — below 40% hp walks quickly to clusters, rapid strikes,
   short wing-assisted leap to reposition.

## Facts for info cards
- Large mantises are known to catch small birds and lizards, though this is rare.
- Hierodula are called "giant Asian mantises" and are among the bulkiest mantis species.
