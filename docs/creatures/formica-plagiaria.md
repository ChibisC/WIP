# Gene Thief ant — *Formica plagiaria* (fictional, game species)

**Invented for the game.** Genus *Formica* is real; the epithet is Latin *plagiaria* (feminine of
*plagiarius*, "kidnapper", the root of "plagiarism" — copying). It stands in for the custom colony that
fields castes of other species. The name was checked against species lists (no real *Formica plagiaria*).

## Biological basis (real behaviour it is modelled on)
- Slave-making (dulotic) *Formica*, especially the blood-red slave-maker *Formica sanguinea*, raid nests of
  other ants (usually *F. fusca*), carry off their pupae and raise them; the captured workers emerge,
  imprint on the raiders' colony odour and work for it. *Polyergus* go further and cannot forage at all.
- So a colony made of several species' workers is real ("mixed colonies"). In the game the plagiarist
  queen's colony raises brood of any chosen species; each borrowed caste looks and behaves like its source.

## Look (modelled on *F. sanguinea*)
- Worker 6–7 mm: *Formica* body plan (single scale petiole, acidopore, 12-segment antennae, large eyes,
  ocelli); head and gaster blackish-brown with a faint bronze sheen; mesosoma and cheeks blood red;
  **notched (emarginate) anterior clypeal margin** — the diagnostic sanguinea feature.
- Queen ~10 mm with a dark red mesosoma, wing scars, large gaster.

## Behaviour
- Native workers: generalists (forage, dig, nurse) with a bite and a short formic acid spray.
- Borrowed castes keep their species' abilities (acid spray, stinging, leaf cutting, aphid tending,
  sickle-jawed fighting) and their own development chamber.
