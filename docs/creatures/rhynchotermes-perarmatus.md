# Sickle-Jaw Termites — *Rhynchotermes perarmatus* (snouted sickle-jaw termite)

Reference for the Sickle-Jaw Termite colony: real anatomy and behaviour, the measurements the models are built from, what the game changes for gameplay, and what was corrected from the original design brief.

- **Species:** *Rhynchotermes perarmatus* (Snyder, 1925). Blattodea: Termitidae: **Syntermitinae** (the "mandibulate nasutes"). Originally described as *Armitermes (Rhynchotermes) perarmatus*; *Armitermes major* Snyder 1925 is a junior synonym. Type locality: Chinilla River, Panama Canal Zone.
- **Genus:** *Rhynchotermes* Holmgren, 1912, about 8 described species, all Neotropical.
- **"Sickle-jaw termite" is a game name.** No standard English common name was found for this species; photographers call it the "armed nasute termite".
- **Verified:** 2026-10-24 against:
  - the Wikipedia pages *Rhynchotermes* and *Syntermitinae*
  - the genus revision by Constantini & Cancello (2016, *Zootaxa* 4109)
  - Scheffrahn's (2019, *ZooKeys* 892) description of *R. armatus*, which compares it with *R. perarmatus*
  - Gil Wizen's field notes and photographs of *R. perarmatus* in Costa Rica

  Items marked **[genus]** are documented for *Rhynchotermes* or Syntermitinae generally, not for this species specifically. **[scaled]** values are *R. armatus* measurements scaled to *R. perarmatus*. **[game]** marks a deliberate gameplay invention.

## 1. Termites are not ants — anatomy shared by every caste

| Feature | Termite (build this) | Ant (do NOT build this) |
|---|---|---|
| Waist | **None.** The thorax joins the abdomen broadly, giving a soft, continuous body. | Narrow petiole/postpetiole waist |
| Antennae | **Straight and beaded** (moniliform), **14 segments** in workers, soldiers and alates; the first and third segments are longer than the second (Wikipedia; Scheffrahn 2019). Soldier antennal segments are long and finely haired. | Elbowed (geniculate) |
| Cuticle | Workers and soldier bodies are soft and translucent. The **worker abdomen is transparent**, so the gut shows through (Wikipedia). | Hard, opaque |
| Legs | Six legs with coxa, trochanter, femur, tibia, tarsus and claws. They are **long and slender** in this genus: the soldier hind tibia is about 0.68 × head-plus-nasus length (*R. armatus*: 1.39 / 2.06 mm). The soldier fore coxa has a **conical, thorn-like process** (Constantini & Cancello 2016). | — |
| Eyes | **Workers and soldiers are blind** and find intruders by chemical cues and touch (Wizen). Only alates and the king and queen have compound eyes and ocelli. The imago's ocelli are kidney-shaped (Constantini & Cancello 2016). | Most castes have eyes |
| Development | Egg → larva → worker, or → presoldier → soldier. **No pupa.** | Egg → larva → pupa → adult |

## 2. Castes of *R. perarmatus*

### Soldier — the signature caste
- **Smaller than the workers** (Wikipedia, genus). Soldiers are monomorphic or weakly dimorphic in the genus (Scheffrahn 2019).
- **Head capsule:** pear-shaped from above and dome-shaped in profile (Constantini & Cancello 2016; Scheffrahn 2019). Colour is **orange to brown-reddish**, glossy and translucent.
  - The reddish head separates it from *R. armatus*, whose head is straw-yellow.
  - Two long apical bristles; the labrum is subrectangular with four bristles.
- **Frontal tube (nasus):**
  - **About 50% longer than the head capsule**, sub-cylindrical, and **projecting slightly downward**.
  - In profile the dorsal margin of the head and of the tube is **concave**.
  - It covers the postclypeus when seen from above and is darker toward the tip.
  - In *R. armatus* the tube is hollow with an even wall, has no setae, and tapers to a **fairly large circular opening**. *R. perarmatus*'s tube is less strongly curved than *armatus*'s.
- **Head length with nasus: 2.5–2.6 mm** (Snyder 1925, quoted by Scheffrahn 2019). The synonym "*major*" had 3.0–3.2 mm.
- **Mandibles:**
  - Long sickles, **about two-thirds the length of the frontal tube**, evenly curved.
  - Each has a **conical marginal tooth** on the inner edge.
  - **When closed, the tips reach well past the opposite mandible**, so they cross (Constantini & Cancello 2016).
  - In *R. armatus* they curve 150–180°, most strongly beyond the marginal tooth, and the marginal teeth angle about 60° toward the labrum.
  - Wizen describes them as double-hooked: they grip tightly and lock in place like a fishhook.
- **Pronotum:** saddle-shaped, with two long bristles on the front lobe margin followed by several short ones.
- **Body:** pale amber-cream and translucent, with brown bands across the abdominal tergites. Legs and antennae are pale yellow-cream.

### Worker
- **Larger than the soldier**, stout. Wizen calls them "clumsy when running".
- **Head:** rounded from above, **flattened top to bottom**, and heavily sclerotised (Wikipedia, genus).
  - Colour: reddish rather than yellow, by contrast with *R. armatus* (Scheffrahn 2019). The game uses a warm tan-amber with a reddish tinge.
  - The **fontanelle is conspicuous, rounded and whitish**, and the cranial suture contrasts (Constantini & Cancello 2016; Scheffrahn 2019).
  - The postclypeus has a medium-dark incomplete line.
- **Mandibles:** chewing, with distinct apical teeth and molar ridges.
- **Abdomen:** transparent, with dark gut contents visible (Wikipedia: "Intestines visible; abdomen transparent").
- **The sister species *R. armatus*** (Scheffrahn 2019, workers, n = 12):
  - head capsule straw-yellow, the same colour as its soldier; a small, faint fontanelle; the postclypeus strongly inflated; 14 antennal articles
  - head 1.17 mm wide and 0.79 mm long to the condyles, so the head is wider than long; hind tibia 1.26 mm
  - *R. perarmatus* differs: its head capsules are reddish and its fontanelle conspicuous
- **Kit v2.2 model (2026-10-25), following the user's brief:**
  - **Size and build:** larger and lankier than the nasute worker, 5.8 mm, with a low, elongated body.
  - **Head:** broad and **flattened top to bottom**: wider than tall, flat on top, rounded seen from above. Warm chestnut-orange `#B9642E` with a distinct whitish fontanelle spot. **No white Y-lines**: the brief drops the contrasting suture so the two species' workers read differently.
  - **Face and mandibles:** the postclypeus is a little inflated, and the mandibles are larger and more visible than the nasute worker's.
  - **Legs, antennae, abdomen:** long slender pale legs and longer antennae; a long, slim, very transparent whitish abdomen with the dark leaf-litter gut showing strongly.
  - The colour is a reconstruction from the brief (reddish per C&C 2016, not the yellow of *R. armatus*).

### Reproductives
- **Imago (alate):**
  - head capsule with a long posterior portion behind the eyes
  - medium-sized eyes and ocelli; ocelli kidney-shaped
  - **four pale spots** on the head about the size of the ocelli
  - tergite bristles mostly on the rear margins; densely haired wings
  - forewing with scale 12.5–14.0 mm; hind tibia about 1.57 mm (Constantini & Cancello 2016)
- **King and queen:** dealates that keep their **wing scales** (stubs). The queen becomes physogastric.
  - Body colour is not given in the sources. Kit v2.1 uses a **reconstruction based on related Syntermitinae**: warm reddish-brown, slightly redder than the nasute royals (see "Royals as modelled" below) **[reconstruction]**.
  - Queen abdomen size is not documented for this species. The game uses the spec's proportions: abdomen 6× head + thorax long and 3.5× the head width wide **[game]**.

### Brood
Eggs and larvae, as in all termites. Larvae are nearly colourless and translucent and grow into workers, or through a presoldier stage into soldiers **[genus]**.

## 3. Behaviour

- **Activity:** most *Rhynchotermes* forage **aboveground in the open on surface litter, at dusk or at night** (Wikipedia; Scheffrahn 2019).
  - For *R. perarmatus*, Eisner (2003) described a **covered foraging trail** with the workers in the centre and the soldiers in a defensive row along it, **mandibles open and frontal tubes raised**. Nickle & Collins (1992) report night foraging.
  - Wizen found a colony moving through **covered tunnels of soil particles** under a heavily decomposed fallen tree. Active tunnels held a thick flow of workers with soldiers scattered along the edges on guard.
- **Habitat:** under decaying bark and rotting logs (Constantini & Cancello 2016). It seems to prefer slightly disturbed habitats such as cleared forest and cattle meadows, and has been found under aged, dry cattle dung (Wizen).
- **Soldier defence:**
  - When a gallery is broken, soldiers pour out to find the intruder while the workers keep working.
  - **Threat display:** a soldier rises onto its legs and **gapes its mandibles** (Wizen's "mute roar").
  - **Bite:** the curved, hooked mandibles grip and lock and are hard to pull free (Wizen).
  - **Secretion:**
    - In Syntermitinae the frontal-gland secretion **cannot be sprayed at a distance**; it is applied by contact (Wikipedia).
    - In *R. perarmatus* it leaves the snout opening as a **sticky, odorous strand** rather than a fluid. It is meant to immobilise enemies, especially ants, and may disrupt ant chemical communication. Soldiers sometimes stick themselves to the ant (Wizen).
    - Many soldiers have **broken snouts**. Wizen suggests the tube may have a breaking point that releases the gland contents quickly.
- **Alarm:** termite soldiers drum or jerk their heads against the substrate **[genus/general]**. This is not specifically documented for *R. perarmatus* in these sources.

## 4. Nest and diet

- **Nest:** underground, or a shallow nest built on the surface (Wikipedia; Scheffrahn 2019). Colonies are found under decaying bark, rotting logs and dung.
- **Diet:** in the genus, mainly **forest leaf litter** (Wikipedia). Wizen's *R. perarmatus* workers carried debris and **compressed wood fibre**. The game uses leaf litter and dead plant matter, the same plant-material resource as the Nasute Termites.

## 6. Appearance used for the game model (from the user's reference photos)

The user supplied four macro photos of *R. perarmatus* by Gil Wizen: side views on a fingertip and on bark, a raised-head front view with mandibles gaping, and a raised-head top view. **They are copyrighted. They are used only to study anatomy and colour, never as textures or in the game.** They are kept in the model kit's `refs/` for side-by-side checks only.

- **Head capsule:**
  - an almost spherical, glossy, glassy translucent dome, about as long as wide, about 1.6× the pronotum width
  - deep orange-red `#B8381E` with bright glassy highlights `#E0603A`; translucent and lighter at the rim, darker toward the base of the nasus
- **Nasus:**
  - grows from the front-top of the head, with a broad base that narrows quickly into a slender tube
  - in profile the top line dips into the base (concave) and runs straight to the tip, curving slightly down
  - dark maroon `#6E1A14` toward the open tip
  - at rest the head is held so the tube points forward and slightly up (Eisner: "frontal tubes raised"). Seen from the front it rises above the mandibles.
- **Mandibles:**
  - long sickles from the lower front corners of the head, hanging forward-down under the nasus
  - each curves about 140° in its swing plane, plus a downward hook at the tip, with a small conical tooth on the inner edge
  - dark red `#7A1E16` shading to near-black tips `#2A0E0A`
  - closed, the tips cross; gaping, they spread sideways like a pair of hooks
- **Thorax:** small and pale orange-cream, with a saddle pronotum and no waist.
- **Abdomen:** oval, amber-cream with **bold brown bands** across each tergite (paler in some individuals) and a dark gut patch. The brief gives `#E8C890` with bands `#9A6A3A`; the model uses the darker `#D49E5C` with bands `#6E3E1E` and pale intersegmental lines `#F5E2B8`, which match the photos under game-like lighting.
- **Legs:** very long and thin, pale yellow-cream and translucent, held with the knees high and the feet spread wide. The brief gives `#E9D49A`; the model uses `#E6CA88`.
- **Antennae:** about as long as head plus nasus. Pale cream oval beads with darker joints and fine hairs.

### Royals as modelled (kit v2.1, 2026-10-25)

Rebuilt to the user's FIX spec (`claude/termite-royals/01-spec-royals-fix.md`). No published description of the *R. perarmatus* imago's colours was found (a 2026-10-25 search turned up only the measurements above), so the **royal colours are a reconstruction based on related Syntermitinae**. They are warm reddish-brown and echo the soldiers' red heads.
- **King:** 8.5 mm (1.45× the worker).
  - **Colour:** head and thorax `#6E3A1E`; abdominal plates `#5A3A22` with a paler membrane `#C8A27A`; legs and antennae tan `#A8784A`; wing scales `#4A2814`.
  - **Shape:** the head is slightly flattened from above like the workers'. The pronotum is broad, flat and shield-like, broader than the nasute's saddle.
  - **Royal features:** triangular wing-scale stubs angled back from the sides of the meso- and metathorax, smoothly bulging compound eyes, two ocelli. The abdomen is modest, about 1.25× head + thorax.
- **Queen:** the king's head and thorax, much smaller relative to her abdomen than before.
  - **Abdomen:** one continuous pleated physogastric body, 22.5 mm long (6× head + thorax) and 5.05 mm wide (3.5× head width).
  - **Rolls:** 10 soft rolls with folded rear lips, deep side creases and shallow top creases. The front dome swells around the back of the thorax, the tip is rounded and slightly pointed, and the belly is flattened.
  - **Plates and dots:** small dark tergite bars `#6A4A2A` on top, paler sternites `#B89060` below, spiracle dots on the sides.
  - **Colour:** glossy, wet and translucent, leaning to the amber/honey end of the age range (default age 0.78), matching the user's side-view queen photo.

## 7. Gameplay adaptations (real → game)

| Topic | Real *R. perarmatus* | Game |
|---|---|---|
| Soldier attack | Bite and lock with hooked mandibles, then apply a sticky secretion from the snout by contact | **Grab-and-squirt:** lunge, hook the enemy between the crossed mandible tips and hold it immobile, then squirt at point-blank range with **ramping damage** (2 → 18 DPS over 6 s). The point-blank delivery stays close to the real contact application; the ramp is **[game]**. |
| Hold | The bite locks; soldiers sometimes stick themselves to ants | The hold ends only when the victim or the soldier dies **[game]**. Allies can free the victim by killing the soldier. |
| Secretion look | A sticky, odorous strand | A thin, clear-to-pale-amber viscous jet that thickens with the ramp, plus droplets, fizz and a faint steam wisp on the victim **[game]** |
| Foraging | Open on surface litter at night (genus), or covered trails with soldiers lining them (*R. perarmatus*) | Open foraging columns; the soldiers gather with the workers, or hold an escort ring round the work area when there is nothing to gather (the defensive rows along the route were removed at the user's request, 2026-11-03). |
| Reproductives | Not documented in detail | One queen and one king in a fixed royal chamber. If the queen dies, the colony is lost (game rule shared with the other species). |
| Food | Leaf litter, debris, wood fibre | The plant-material resource shared with the Nasute Termites |
| Death | — | Termite corpses are food for ant colonies |
