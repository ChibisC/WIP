# Megarian banded centipede — *Scolopendra cingulata* (Scolopendromorpha) — BOSS

## Size
- 100–150 mm (game boss: 180 mm, ~1.3×).

## Anatomy
- Long, flattened body of **21 leg-bearing segments → 21 pairs of legs**.
- Head shield rounded, short antennae of ~17–20 segments (≈ .1 L);
  4 ocelli each side; huge **forcipules** curving inward under the head with
  black tips (venom claws).
- Tergites broad, each with paramedian sutures; **terminal (ultimate) legs**
  long, thick, spiny, held backward and slightly spread — used to grip and
  as a decoy head.

## Colour ("cingulata" = banded)
- Body olive-yellow to orange-tan with a **dark greenish/black band at the
  rear edge of every tergite**, producing strong banding; head and first
  tergite reddish-orange; legs yellow. Glossy (clearcoat).

## Movement
- Metachronal leg waves; sinuous lateral undulation of the body when fast;
  head scans side to side; can rear the front third up.

## Behaviour (boss phases)
1. **Coil & strike**: coils the body, then lunges and bites with forcipules
   (venom DoT), telegraphed by the head rising and drawing back.
2. **Body whip / thrash**: lashes the rear half sideways, sweeping ants away
   (knockback arc), then grips with the ultimate legs.
3. **Frenzy**: below 35% hp moves faster, rears and bites repeatedly,
   coils around clusters (constricting area damage).

## Facts for info cards
- Scolopendra hunt insects, spiders and even small lizards using venom that acts in seconds.
- The long rear legs can pinch and are held up to look like a second head.
