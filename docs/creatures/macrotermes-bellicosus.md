# Fungus-growing termite — *Macrotermes bellicosus* (Termitidae)

## Castes and size
- Workers (minor/major) ~3.5–4.5 mm. Soldiers: minor ~5 mm, major ~8–10 mm.
- Physogastric queen up to ~110 mm (largest known termite queen).

## Anatomy (very different from ants)
- **No narrow waist**: thorax joins the abdomen broadly; soft, cylindrical,
  segmented abdomen.
- **Antennae straight and beaded (moniliform)**, not elbowed, ~17–19 bead-like
  segments.
- Workers: rounded pale head, small mandibles, **blind** (no compound eyes).
- **Soldiers**: large, dark, heavily sclerotised, elongate rectangular head
  (head ≈ .45 L), long **sabre-like crossing mandibles** with dark tips;
  pale soft body behind the armoured head. Blind.
- Legs short, stout (hind ≈ .55 L).

## Colour and surface
- Workers **pale cream, soft and translucent** — gut contents show darker
  through the abdomen (brown for litter feeders). Matte-satin.
- Soldiers: head orange-brown to dark reddish-brown, glossy; body cream.
- Translucency/subsurface look: transmission/thickness in material.

## Movement
- Slow, deliberate; workers shuffle in dense streams under covered runways.
- **Soldiers bang their heads against the substrate** (vibratory drumming in
  bursts) to signal alarm, which recruits other soldiers and makes workers
  retreat.

## Behaviour
- Build huge mounds of clay/soil with ventilation chimneys; cultivate
  *Termitomyces* fungus on combs made from faeces.
- Soldiers defend breaches with mandibles; majors block tunnels with heads.
- Ants (e.g., Matabele ants, Megaponera) raid termites as prey.
- Game: rival colony / wave enemy; workers weak, soldiers armoured biters,
  head-banging spreads alarm.

## Facts for info cards
- Macrotermes mounds can stand several metres tall and are ventilated like lungs.
- Termite soldiers drum their heads on the ground to send vibration alarms through the nest.
- Termites are more closely related to cockroaches than to ants.
